# Product Guidelines

## Brand & Voice
- **Tone**: Professional, clear, and efficient.
- **Audience**: Hardware store staff and administrators who require a reliable and fast operational tool.
- **Language**: Spanish (as seen in the application terminology).

## UX/UI Principles
- **Design System**: Bootstrap 5 for a clean, responsive, and familiar interface.
- **Interactions**: Fast and dynamic utilizing HTMX to avoid full page reloads for common actions like adding to cart, updating items, etc.
- **Feedback**: Clear visual cues for stock shortages (e.g., greyed out badges) and straightforward forms.
- **Accessibility**: Simple navigation structure with distinct tabs and modals to reduce cognitive load.
